How this desk works

Thirty-eight decisions across four rounds. You are the officer assessing them.

  1. Every correct call imposes one condition on the file. Three or more in a row and each call imposes two.
  2. A wrong call imposes nothing, and your streak resets to zero.
  3. Score is 10 points a call, doubled at a streak of three, quadrupled at a streak of six.
  4. Reach 48 conditions and the file is fully protected. Anything you get wrong comes back for review at the end.
